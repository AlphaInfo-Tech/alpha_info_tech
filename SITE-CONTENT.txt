--- HERO ---
Eyebrow: Why We Started
Heading: Built to fix our own problems first

Alpha Info Tech didn't start as a software company with a formal office
and a product roadmap. It started because we were running a small
business ourselves, and the software available to us didn't fit —
everything on the market was oversized, overpriced, and built for a
company we weren't.

--- THE STORY ---
/01 — The Problem
We were losing business we should have won

Enquiries came in over WhatsApp and got buried in the chat within a
day. Follow-ups slipped. We didn't need a CRM with fifty features we'd
never touch — we needed to stop losing leads we'd already earned.

/02 — The First Fix
A WhatsApp enquiry tracker, built for us

So we built one. A simple system to track enquiries coming in over
WhatsApp. In its first week alone, it recovered four leads that would
otherwise have fallen through the cracks. Then we built a quotation
generator, because sending quotes fast was costing us deals too.

/03 — The Turning Point
A colleague asked for the same setup

Every tool we built solved a problem we were actually living through —
not a theoretical one. When a colleague running their own business saw
what we'd put together and asked for the same setup, that request
became the start of Alpha Info Tech's module library, long before any
of it was for sale.

--- CLOSING QUOTE ---
"That's still how we build today. Every module in our library exists
because someone on our own team hit that exact wall first — which
means what you get from us has already been tested where it matters
most: in a real, small business, under real pressure."
— The Alpha Info Tech Team
